# Giant yellow cat mount

`giant_cat_mount.bbmodel` is the editable Blockbench project. All vanilla UVs and the embedded 64×32 texture are retained. The two tail cubes are intentionally resized to 130% thickness and 75% length (1.3 × 6 × 1.3 model pixels per segment); other cube dimensions remain unchanged. The generated copy corrects the tail joint: `group4`/`tail2` become descendants of `tail1`, their pivot is the resized base's distal cap center, and the tip is translated to that joint. The tip's local rest X angle becomes −47.4338° so its inherited rest angle stays −99°. A volume-only `tail1_volume` child lets rest thicken the base another 20% without scaling the tip twice or moving the joint; the tip receives its own matching transverse scale. Original files in `source/` are never rewritten.

The clips are defined once in `tools/export-giant-cat-mount.mjs`, then exported both to this Blockbench project and to the Forge/NeoForge runtime JSON. Blockbench contains 60 Hz linear preview samples of the runtime's zero-tangent cubic Hermite curves, so editing and in-game playback closely agree. Rebuild with `node tools/export-giant-cat-mount.mjs`; verify with `node tests/giant-cat-mount-assets.mjs` and `node tests/giant-cat-mount-exporter.mjs`. The default build reads `source/` without modifying it. `--source path/to/model.bbmodel` selects another model for a one-off export without replacing the archived original.

The mount renderer supplies the size multiplier. Do not enlarge the project geometry or resample its vanilla texture. Runtime animation is applied as local per-bone transforms, with a grounded, server-speed-driven walk/run blend, synced jump anticipation, air/landing timing, and subtle turn lean. Run root lift is at most 0.18 model pixels and authored pitch at most 1°; softness comes primarily from body and leg-local squash/stretch. Physical jump height belongs to the carrier.

Five clips are exported: `idle`, `walk`, `run`, `jump`, and reversible `rest`. Idle sway is on the torso child, not shared leg ancestors, so the paws remain planted. Vanilla sitting/ordered-sit state smoothly drives side-lying rest: a 90° longitudinal roll, forelegs extending side-forward, and relaxed staggered hind paws. Leg extension blends with the roll so shafts and paws remain visible outside the torso. Dense root-height keys are derived from the transformed model vertices to keep contact through the roll instead of allowing intermediate floor penetration. The live renderer locks body heading throughout rest and recovery, independently observes nearby players/living entities within ±25° yaw and ±10° pitch, and attaches career headwear using the same animated head ancestry. World-aligned quaternion compensation prevents the rolled head from confusing horizontal look with vertical tilt. `node tests/giant-cat-animation-sampler.mjs` compiles and exercises both real Java samplers; asset tests sample transformed tail endpoints, idle paw corners and side-contact geometry across the curves. `node tests/giant-rest-look.mjs` checks target selection and compensated head rotation. This asset pack does not itself create an entity or renderer.
